### R2: Supervised playback and native control

WHEN a completed answer is selected for speech THEN one identified request SHALL be owned until its terminal outcome. Stop SHALL discard unsent sections and hush playback independently of provider cancellation. Replay SHALL create a new identity. Failed or uncertain outcomes SHALL be visible and SHALL NOT retry automatically.

**Acceptance:**
- Stop before submission and stop during playback cannot leave queued speech audible
- Completion after a stop cannot claim uninterrupted playback
- Replay and source changes cannot redirect an earlier request
- Disabled voice starts no audio task
- A stalled hush write or receipt cannot hold the TUI past the owner-ruled five-second deadline; expiry is unknown
- An untagged error after admission cannot terminate identified playback
- A complete-playback receipt after a stop request retains the request in its outcome and UI wording
- Turn input sources and state reset are structurally separated so run.rs stays within the 500 production-line policy; execution behaviour and existing tests are unchanged (Waffles wall ruling, 9 September 2026 15:15 Melbourne).
