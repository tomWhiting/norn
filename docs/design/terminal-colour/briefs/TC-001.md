# TC-001: Start the TUI when colour capabilities are uncertain

> **Cluster:** terminal-colour
> **Checklist:**
> - C1 — Colour uncertainty never rejects the TUI and all output respects the selected depth.
> **Stories:**
> - S1 (Operator, Terminal user) — I can launch Norn on a remote terminal without manually repairing colour environment variables.

## Purpose

Waffles reports Tom’s Ghostty TERM was refused on the 205. On 9 September 2026 at 14:07 and 14:09 Melbourne, Waffles ruled: start, degrade, explain once; never refuse over colour.

## Task

Replace the colour admission gate with explicit rendering depth. Keep cursor/raw-mode I/O failures real. No extra subprocess or startup probe. Checked source evidence and unrun live-terminal limits are recorded in [PROOF.md](../PROOF.md).

## Requirements

### R1: Progressive colour from startup through every renderer

WHEN extended colour is unconfirmed THEN Norn SHALL retain its interactive TUI and render using 16 ANSI colours or default colours, with one in-interface notice. Recognized modern TERM suffixes SHALL support indexed colour without a terminfo requirement.

**Acceptance:**
- xterm-ghostty, xterm-kitty, alacritty and wezterm start without COLORTERM
- Explicit truecolor/24bit COLORTERM wins; otherwise unknown TERM degrades to ANSI16 and unset, empty or dumb TERM uses default colours
- No colour-only fallback into print mode
- Retained transcript foreground/background and Iridium composer obey the same depth
- Selection and tool emphasis remain visible without custom colours
- Strict workspace Clippy, fmt, focused tests and venue review before landing
