### R2: Explicit, bounded admission with one recipient and source generations

The host SHALL create one recipient-scoped inbox with explicit positive retained-count and retained-byte budgets and explicit source policies Hold, NextTurn or Wake. The synchronous MCP reader SHALL attempt admission without awaiting inbox capacity. All staged, held, queued and claimed-but-unconsumed messages SHALL count against the same budgets until explicitly consumed or denied. A full inbox SHALL report a typed refusal and remain observable while outstanding RPC responses continue. Connecting a replacement SHALL NOT retire the current source until the host activates the new connection. Retirement SHALL fence subsequent emissions from the old connection. Already admitted messages SHALL retain their original source generation. Cancellation of delivery SHALL leave its message retained. No implicit durable admission or upstream acknowledgement SHALL be claimed.

**Acceptance:**
- Two callers sharing a client cannot acquire two inbox receivers; the intended recipient is captured at host construction.
- Counts and bytes remain charged when an owner claims a message; dropping the delivery claim leaves it available again.
- Holding messages cannot expand an unbounded side vector or free quota.
- A pending/new connection stages events; only explicit activation admits them for delivery and fences its predecessor.
- Full-count and full-byte refusals are visible in typed status and logs, and neither awaits nor blocks the sole RPC reader.
- A retired connection cannot publish to the current recipient generation.
- Wake readiness is push-driven and cancel-safe; Hold and NextTurn alone do not wake an idle session.
- Receiver closure refuses new events visibly; zero limits are validation errors, and this API defines no numeric defaults.

### R4a: Running-session owned delivery with exact persistence and push readiness

WHEN the existing running LoopContext has its authoritative agent UUID, the owner SHALL install one explicitly bounded MCP channel inbox and receive its host handle. Setup SHALL admit eligible Wake and NextTurn messages as host-framed external UserMessage events without invoking local slash-command or permission-response parsing. Busy and linger boundaries SHALL claim Wake messages only, after every running tool has a result. One prepared event with stable namespaced identity, timestamp and parent SHALL remain attached to its retained delivery claim through append failure; consumption SHALL follow exact idempotent persistence synchronously before cancellation-capable hooks. Charge SHALL remain until consumption. Idle readiness SHALL be push-driven and cancellation-safe. Source messages SHALL not alter model, output schema, recipient or approval authority. This row SHALL NOT connect CLI servers or claim a complete Channels launch feature; runtime publication and operator wiring remain following rows. After the persisted append and consumption, the loop SHALL emit a distinct typed McpChannel delivery observation through the existing bounded AgentEvent broadcast before hooks. It SHALL carry host source/generation/recipient/sequence, raw external text and the exact persisted event ID. No synthetic operator prompt, forged inter-agent lifecycle or second queue SHALL be added.

**Acceptance:**
- Installed inbox recipient matches the actual LoopContext.agent_id; absent identity and duplicate install fail by name, and later mismatched identity cannot deliver.
- A channel wake uses persisted external event identity without an empty operator prompt; /exit, /model and approval-looking content remain attributed untrusted content.
- Wake arriving during a tool batch reaches the next request only after tool completion; NextTurn arriving during a run remains until an independently started turn.
- Held work stays retained and cannot wake or deliver. Existing linger selects directly on channel readiness without a new polling path.
- Count and bytes remain charged through claimed/prepared state and sink failure. Exact event retry reconciles an ambiguous append without creating a second UserMessage.
- Cancellation after content persistence but during hooks cannot redeliver the message; cancellation before persistence leaves work retained.
- Source metadata cannot alter the request output schema, model or host approval state.
- Row code uses existing session ownership and EventStore; no daemon, Aion dependency or new durable ingress service is required.
- A distinct typed channel observation carries the matching persisted UserMessage ID and host source; publication precedes cancellation-capable hooks and Debug does not expose external content.
- Existing exhaustive provider-event regressions explicitly classify McpChannel observations as unexpected when only provider or subagent events are expected; no wildcard hides newly introduced variants.
